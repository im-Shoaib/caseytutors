Taught by Dr Dulangi SJ

Dr Dulangi S. Jayasundera is a PhD‑qualified VCE Biology educator, former research scientist and experienced teacher with First Class Honours and over 13 years of VCE Biology teaching experience in Victoria.

She specialises in VCE Biology Units 1 to 4, including early VCE Biology preparation for Grade 10 students, Unit 1 & 2 foundation support, Unit 3 & 4 theory lessons, SAC preparation, topic tests, exam‑style questions and final exam revision.

Her teaching focuses on helping students understand Biology clearly, apply knowledge to unfamiliar questions and write answers in the way VCAA examiners expect.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this for Grade 10, Year 11 or Year 12 students?

Yes. Casey Tutors supports Grade 10 students preparing early for VCE Biology, Year 11 students studying Units 1 & 2, and Year 12 students studying Units 3 & 4.

No. Casey Tutors offers structured group classes only. Individual lessons are not available.

What is included in the Unit 3/4 program?

Students receive theory teaching, quiz and SAC practice, topic tests, notes, learning portal support, progress feedback and revision support.

Are classes available online?

Yes. Students can attend from Narre Warren, Mount Waverley or Online.

Who teaches the VCE Biology program?
